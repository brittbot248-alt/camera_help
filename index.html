    💾 Where recordings go
      

    • Micro SD card: Vyanet supplies a 32GB Micro SD card in the camera. Cards up to 512GB are supported (the D1 comes with its 32GB card already installed).
      • 

    • Vyanet cloud: 7 days of recording. Cloud recording uses the cloud codes included with your Vyanet QA plan (Bronze 1, Silver 2, Gold 3, Platinum 4); extra codes are $5.00 per month per camera. See Cloud pricing in Common questions · Request additional cloud codes. If you request cloud codes, Vyanet Socworks will email them to you. Redeem them in the Vyanet app under Account > “Activation Codes”.
      • 

    • Vyanet N85 NVR: the cameras can record to a Vyanet recorder (NVR); for the D1, contact Vyanet Socworks for details. For longer, more robust storage, Vyanet suggests adding the Vyanet N85 NVR (hard drive up to 16TB). The N85 comes with a hard drive already installed and records up to 8 cameras. The Plus (+) Plan adds the Vyanet NVR to any QA plan for $10 more per month.
      • 

    • When storage fills up, the camera automatically records over the oldest footage.
